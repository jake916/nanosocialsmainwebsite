## **Homepage: The shift**

*Opens with the problem, then brings Nano in as the mechanism.*

**Eyebrow:** The old way is loud and blind.

**Heading:** You paid the influencer. Then what?

**Body:** Most brands spend on creators the same way. Find a big account, send a fee, hope. The post goes up, the likes roll in, and when it is over you are left holding a screenshot and a feeling. No line of sight into who actually moved anything. No way to run it again on purpose.

Nano changes the order. A brand opens a campaign and sets the brief. Matched creators pick it up. They post, in their own voice, to their own people. Every view, comment and share lands back in one place, tied to the creator who earned it. You watch the campaign happen. Then you run the next one sharper.

## **Homepage: How it works**

*The Collabstr pattern, rebuilt: the whole arc from brief to payout, shown as one flow. Numbered so it can lay out as five connected steps.*

**Heading:** From brief to payout, in one place.

1. **Open a campaign.** Set the brief, the platforms, how many creators, the budget. Pick content, traffic, or both.  
2. **Creators pick it up.** Nano matches your brief to graded creators and drafts a first script in each one's voice. They accept, you approve.  
3. **They post.** Rolling or launch-day. Every post is tracked from the moment it goes live.  
4. **You watch it move.** Views, reach, engagement and clicks, per creator, as they land.  
5. **Everyone gets paid.** Payouts split automatically when the work is in. No chasing, no spreadsheets.

## **Homepage: What are you here to do?**

*The SideShift goal-picker, rebuilt around Nano's real campaign types. Three selectable cards.*

**Heading:** What are you here to do?

**Card 1, Get people talking.** Content campaigns. Creators post about your brand and make it a thing worth talking about. You pay a set rate per creator, by grade. Best for launches, moments, and getting a name into the culture.

**Card 2, Drive the click.** Traffic campaigns. Creators send qualified people to your link. You pay per verified click, roughly ₦20 to ₦30 each, with no content to manage. Best for sign-ups, downloads and sales.

**Card 3, Send them out.** *(coming soon)* Nano Missions. Creators go where the work is: a shoot on location, a visit to your store, a real task with logistics on you. Best for brands that need presence, not just posts.

*Note: a click pool can ride on top of a content campaign, so one wave can do both.*

## **Page: For Brands**

**Hero heading:** Your campaign. Our Platform.

**Subhead:** Everything you need to run creator campaigns on purpose: graded creators, briefs in their own voice, live numbers, automatic payouts. One platform, start to finish.

**Pick creators by what they move, not who they are.** Every creator on Nano is graded G1 to G5 on the things that matter: views, comments, likes, shares, reach. Follower count counts for the least. You are buying performance, not a vanity number.

**Two ways to run it.** Content campaigns for conversation. Traffic campaigns for clicks. Attach a click pool to a content wave and do both at once.

**Set the rules, then let it run.** Prep windows  of 3, 5, 7 or 14 days. One cycle or many. Public, so matched creators get auto-invited, or private, by invite only. Rolling posting, or a locked launch day. You decide, the engine handles the rest.

**See the campaign, not a summary.** Every post measured on its own, from the day it goes live, per creator, in real time. The report is the product, not an afterthought.

**Pricing, plainly.** Content campaigns run about ₦45,000 per creator, set by grade. Traffic campaigns run ₦20 to ₦30 per verified click. Nano adds a 7.5% platform fee, capped at ₦500,000. No retainers, no lock-in.

**CTA:** Open a campaign
