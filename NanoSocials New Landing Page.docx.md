# **3\. The problem Nano solves**

**People skip ads. They don’t skip people they trust.**

People scroll past ads. They always have, and spending more only buys you more   
of the same result.  
Creator marketing is different. Creators hold attention that ads cannot buy, because their audience trusts and listens to them. Now, when several creators talk about the same thing, it stops looking like an ad and starts looking like a recommendation. One voice is chill but many voices build a wall of social proof for your brand. 

**So why doesn’t every brand just run a dozen creators at once?**

Because it is hard to pull off alone. Brands spend real money making content, then it lives or dies as one post, to one audience.

Getting that content in front of people, across many accounts, at the same time, is a scheduling headache.

Managing one creator is hard. Now, managing ten, with briefs, timing, approvals and payments, is a second job.

# **5\. How Nano works**

**From brief to coordinated reach.**

1. **Create your brand workspace** — one place for campaigns, creators and results; agencies can run several brand accounts.  
2. **Tell us your goal** — pick a creator or traffic campaign, describe it in a sentence, Nano drafts the creator brief, you edit or regenerate.  
3. **Add your brand assets** — product shots, logos or a sample video, so creators post on-brand from day one.  
4. **Set your timeline** — prep time, run dates, who can join, and rolling vs launch-day posting.  
5. **Pick your creator mix** — platforms and how many at each grade G1 to G5, graded on engagement not followers, budget adding up live.  
6. **Fund and launch** — review, top up the wallet, go live, add a click pool if you want, unspent budget comes back.  
7. **Creators come in and post** — auto-invited matches or your own via private link, you approve each piece against the brief.  
8. **Track it as it happens** — one dashboard for active campaigns, creators working, views, reach and engagement.  
9. **Learn, then run it again** — see what each creator delivered, keep the ones who moved people, launch the next cycle.

# **7\. How creators are graded**

**One grade. Two ways to read it.**

Every creator earns a grade, from G1 to G5. This is based on real engagement, how much your audience interacts with your content, never on a follower number alone.

**What the grade means for brands**

You can see the level of every creator before you spend a naira.  
 A higher grade means a stronger, proven track record of moving people.  
 Your package mixes grades on purpose, so your budget buys a balance of fresh reach and proven engagement, not just on one postt.

**What the grade means for creators**

Your grade is earned, never bought.  
Make content that moves people and you climb through the tiers, whatever your follower count. A tight, loyal audience can take you further than a huge one that gets ignored. The more campaigns you do, the more you earn.

**The ladder**

**G1**  Starting out. Building a track record.

**G2**  Finding a rhythm. A steady response from the audience.

**G3**  Proven. Reliably moves the people who follow them.

**G4**  Strong. A real pull with their community.

**G5**  Top tier. Moves people at the highest level.

Creator fees rise with the grade. The exact amounts are in the pricing section and in your quote.

**Same grade, read two ways.**

The brand reads it as proof. The creator reads it as a ladder. That is the deal Nano keeps on both sides, and it is why nobody here is paying for, or chasing, follower counts.

# **15\. Questions people ask (FAQs)**

**What is Nanosocials?**

A platform brands use to run marketing campaigns with many creators at once, manage the work in one place, and track how it performs. Creators are measured by real engagement, not follower count.

**Is Nano an influencer marketing agency?**

No. An agency hands you one name and runs it for you. Nano is a platform: you run a campaign across many creators, each posting to the people who trust them, with the results laid out for you.

**How does Nano help brands?**

It organises a creator campaign around one brief, gets many creators posting around the same time, and shows you how each one performed so your next campaign is smarter.

**Can creators earn through Nano?**

Yes. Creators get matched with brand campaigns, deliver the agreed work, and get paid for it. Earnings depend on the campaign and the creator’s grade, not on follower count.

**Do I need a big following to join as a creator?**

No. Your grade is based on how much your audience responds to your work, not how many followers you have. A smaller, engaged audience can take you a long way.

**What does a campaign cost?**

It depends on the package and the number of creators. Awareness packages start at ₦451,500 for 10 creators and 10 videos. You always see the full scope and price in your quote before you commit.

**Can I use the content in my own ads?**

Only within the rights you agree for the campaign. Confirm paid use, the channels and how long you can run it before you use a creator’s content as an ad.

**Does a campaign guarantee sales?**

No. Nano gets you attention and shows you how it performed. Any specific target is written into your agreement, along with how it will be measured.

**How do I get started?**

Brands can start a campaign or book a demo. Creators can register to join the network. Pick your path above.
